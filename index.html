So what

Greenhouse vegetable prices are driven mainly by season and energy cost — winter commands a real premium because field-grown competitors are out of season, and every dollar of heating cost measurably passes through into the price growers charge.

The seasonal price curve

Price relative to January, holding everything else constant. This single pattern is the largest source of explained variance in the entire model.

Monthly price change vs January: Feb -2.24, Mar -1.74, Apr -4.13, May -5.51, Jun -6.10, Jul -5.74, Aug -7.20, Sep -6.07, Oct -4.47, Nov -2.60, Dec -1.18.
